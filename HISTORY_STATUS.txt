TO SEE THE HISTORY FEATURE:

1. ⚙️  Reload Extension
   └─ Go to chrome://extensions/
   └─ Click "Reload" button on SmartLens AI
   
2. 📝 Create Some History
   └─ Select text on any webpage
   └─ Click extension icon
   └─ Choose a mode (Summarize/Explain/etc)
   └─ Click "Analyze Selected Text"
   └─ Wait for result
   
3. 👀 View History
   └─ Open extension popup again
   └─ Scroll down to "📜 Analysis History" section
   └─ You should see your analysis listed!

4. 🧪 Test Actions
   └─ Click "📋 Copy" to copy result
   └─ Click "👁️ View" to see full result
   └─ Click "🗑️ Delete" to remove entry
   └─ Click "🗑️ Clear" to remove all
